About me

I'm Peace Chisom Obisike, a final-year law student who has spent the last two years inside working law firms rather than only in lecture halls. That's where I learned what actually eats an attorney's day: not the law, but the research nobody has time to run, the drafts that sit half-done, the deadlines that live in someone's head.

I bring a law student's training and a virtual assistant's discipline: care with detail, a calm way with a full inbox, and the habit of asking one clear question early rather than guessing. I don't give legal advice or client-facing opinions; that judgment stays with the lawyer. Confidentiality is non-negotiable and I sign an NDA before any file changes hands.

Outside client work I'm completing arbitration training and served as Campus Director of the Law Students' Association of Nigeria, where I was named Campus Director of the Year 2025.
