What I take off your desk

Everything below is the work that needs care and a legal eye but not a licensed attorney's hour.

Document drafting & review

First drafts and clean-ups of routine documents from your templates and instructions, formatted and proofread so they arrive ready for your review.

  • Engagement letters, demand letters, correspondence
  • Contracts, NDAs and agreements from precedent
  • Pleadings, motions and discovery documents (formatting, exhibits, citation checks)

Legal research & memos

Focused research on a defined question, summarised in a short memo with sources cited so you can verify quickly.

  • Case law and statute look-ups
  • Summaries of judgments, regulations and guidance
  • Comparison notes across US states or UK jurisdictions

Case & calendar management

Deadlines, court dates and file organisation kept current in your practice management system, with reminders before anything falls due.

  • Docketing and deadline tracking
  • Matter set-up and file organisation in Clio / MyCase
  • Billing entries, time capture and invoice preparation

Client intake & communication

A professional first point of contact for prospective and existing clients, so enquiries are answered the same day and nothing sits in the inbox.

  • Intake calls and questionnaires, conflict checks
  • Email and inbox management, appointment scheduling
  • Client status updates and follow-ups

General practice support

The administrative layer that keeps a small firm running smoothly.

  • Transcription and note-taking
  • Data entry, spreadsheets and reporting
  • Presentation and document formatting
